# 📆 Daily Study Plan

**Purpose:** the engine behind the 8:00 AM daily nudge. Two moving parts: **which phase you're in** (from the roadmap timeline) and **what kind of work today calls for** (from the weekly rhythm below). Together they answer "what do I actually do today?" without needing a big planning session every morning.

## Weekly Rhythm (Network+ phase — Mon–Fri only)

Every weekday has a job. Weekends are fully off. This keeps study sessions varied instead of just re-reading the same material on repeat.

| Day | Focus | What it looks like |
|---|---|---|
| **Monday** | 🧠 New material | Learn something new in the current phase's cert — a video module, a new domain of the exam objectives. |
| **Tuesday** | 🧠 New material / practice | Continue new content and quiz yourself on Monday's material. Wrong answers get a note — that's the real signal of what to restudy. |
| **Wednesday** | 🛠️ Hands-on lab | Apply it for real — in the homelab, Packet Tracer, a cloud free-tier sandbox, or Linux CLI. This is the step that makes it stick and gives you something to add to the portfolio. |
| **Thursday** | 🧠 New material / weak-area review | Continue new content, or circle back to whatever earlier practice exposed as shaky. |
| **Friday** | ⏱️ Timed practice + portfolio note | A timed practice drill — builds the exam-day muscle — plus a quick note logged for the portfolio. |
| **Saturday–Sunday** | 🌱 Off | No studying obligation. Rest, or optionally skim next week's topics if you want a head start — never required. |

Once CCNA and later phases begin, this weekly rhythm (still Mon–Fri) carries forward the same way.

## How the Daily Message Works

Every morning at **8:00 AM Central**, a scheduled message:

1. Figures out which **phase** today falls in (from the timeline above, based on the date).
2. Figures out which **day of the week** it is (from the rhythm above).
3. Combines them into one concrete task for today — cert name + task type + a specific suggestion tied to the homelab where relevant.
4. Throws in a bit of momentum/encouragement, because a 6-month plan is a marathon.

No setup needed on your end — just show up and do what it says. If a day gets missed, no penalty; the next message just picks up from wherever the calendar says you should be.
